# DevOps Career & Behavioral Round — Cheat Sheet

## The one-liners

| Concept | One line |
|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself | Present, past, future — about a minute |
| Why DevOps | The real moment it clicked, not a slogan |
| Why this role | One specific reason about this company |
| STAR | Situation, task, action, result — under two minutes |
| Outage story | Fix in 30 seconds, guardrail gets the last word |
| Disagreement | Argue with reasons, compromise, commit |
| Learning a tool | Smallest real thing first, break it, fix it |
| Weakness | Real weakness, real cost, concrete fix in progress |
| Prioritisation | User impact first, blast radius second, say it out loud |
| 30-60-90 | Learn first, contribute second, own third |
| Why hire you | Two or three real strengths matched to their problems |

## STAR, at a glance

| Part | What to say | Keep it to |
|---|---|---|
| Situation | The context in one sentence | 15 seconds |
| Task | Your role, not the team's | 10 seconds |
| Action | The steps you took, in order | 45 seconds |
| Result | The outcome with a number + the guardrail | 30 seconds |

## Five stories to prepare

| Story | Answers |
|---|---|
| An outage you fixed | Incident handling, prioritisation, calm under pressure |
| A disagreement | Teamwork, losing gracefully, committing |
| A project you're proud of | Technical depth, ownership, impact |
| A mistake you made | Honesty, learning, the fix |
| A deadline crunch | Prioritisation, communication, trade-offs |

## Common questions, one-line answers

| Question | The shape of a good answer |
|---|---|
| Tell me about yourself | Present → past → future, ~60 seconds |
| Why DevOps? | A real moment + what keeps you in it |
| Why this company? | One specific, researched reason |
| Biggest weakness? | Real weakness + cost + fix in progress |
| Where in 2–3 years? | Scope, not titles — deeper work, mentoring |
| Salary expectations? | A researched range, decided before the call |
| Do you have questions for us? | Three real ones — on-call, last incident, success at 6 months |

## The 30-60-90 shape

| Phase | Focus | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days | Learn | Architecture, pipeline, on-call, owners; small fixes |
| 60 days | Contribute | Own a flaky pipeline or a missing alert |
| 90 days | Own | Ship one reliability improvement; join on-call |

## Red flags in your own answers

- [ ] A weakness that is secretly a strength ("I work too hard")
- [ ] A story where the team did everything and you watched
- [ ] An outage story that ends at the fix, with no guardrail
- [ ] "I love your culture" with nothing specific behind it
- [ ] A 2–3 year answer that is just a job title
- [ ] No questions for them at the end

## Before the round, check

- [ ] 60-second intro timed under 75 seconds
- [ ] Five STAR stories, each under two minutes
- [ ] Three resume bullets rewritten with numbers
- [ ] Salary range researched from three sources, target + walk-away written down
- [ ] Three questions to ask them, none answered by the posting
- [ ] One real tool you learned recently, with how long it took
